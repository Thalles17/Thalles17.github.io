Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy describes how Casemate Publisher (“the app”), a private tool operated by The Casemate, handles data. The app is used only by the owner of The Casemate YouTube channel to manage that channel's own videos.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies to data processed by Google.

What data the app accesses

  • Basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm the authorized channel.
  • The channel's own videos, to upload new videos and to update their title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy status and scheduled publish time.

The app does not access, collect or store data about viewers, subscribers, comments or any other YouTube user.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to perform the upload and update actions requested by the channel owner.
  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • No cookies or tracking are used by the app or by this website.

Retention and deletion

Stored tokens are kept until the channel owner deletes them or revokes access. You can revoke the app's access at any time at Google Account → Third-party apps with account access. Revoking access makes the stored tokens unusable; the local token file is then deleted.
